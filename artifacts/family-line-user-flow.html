Which part is the screen, the voice agent, or the backend?

The family interface

A conversation for sharing information and confirming what gets saved. An Overview for requests and the latest call. Sources, history and settings stay available when needed.

The phone conversation

Guava handles the live exchange. The caller begins with ordinary words. The assistant reads relevant context, asks about missing details, gives one step and listens to the result.

The shared backend

Python controls reads and confirmed writes. SQLite keeps approved information, source references, conversations, outcomes and follow-ups. Family notes provide context for a later call.
